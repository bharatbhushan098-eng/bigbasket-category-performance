Three categories clear their targets: Household Essentials, Personal Care, and Bakery. Dairy & Eggs is below target but within the 15% watch band. Snacks & Beverages and Fruits & Vegetables are below target by more than 15%, so they are Critical.

## Two concrete recommendations

1. **Prioritize Snacks & Beverages and Fruits & Vegetables for corrective action.** They are the two Critical categories, short by ₹2,105 and ₹2,210 respectively. Review product mix, availability, and order volume in these categories first rather than cutting resources from the three categories already above target.

2. **Protect and learn from Household Essentials performance.** Household Essentials is ₹4,715 above its ₹17,000 target and is the strongest category by Delivered revenue. Use its high-performing product/supplier mix as a benchmark for diagnosing why the Critical categories are under target.
